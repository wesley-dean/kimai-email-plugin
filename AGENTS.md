# Agent Instructions

Before modifying governed content in this repository, read the repository
documentation and the standards under `doc/standards/` that apply to the work.

Files under `doc/standards/` are governing project requirements, not
suggestions.  Apply every relevant standard unless an accepted
repository-specific ADR or explicit repository policy supersedes or refines it.
Do not silently deviate from a governing standard.

Presence does not imply applicability.  General and cross-cutting standards
apply where relevant, and a language-specific standard applies to maintained
content in that language.  Content under `doc/standards/examples/` is
illustrative and non-normative unless a governing standard explicitly states
otherwise.

Repository-specific accepted ADRs and explicit local policies take precedence
when they refine or supersede an imported standard.  Do not edit imported
standards locally to encode exceptions; record project-specific exceptions in
repository governance.

Before consequential work, review `doc/adr/README.md`, the relevant ADRs, and
`doc/decisions.md`.
